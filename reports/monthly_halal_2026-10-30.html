How to read this report (start here)

This is a daily scoreboard for 22 companies and funds (halal-screened view). Every day the engine records their prices and numbers, gives each one a score from 0 to 100 on four things, and ranks them from best to worst overall.

  • Value – how cheap the price looks compared with the others (near the low end of its yearly range, lower P/E ratio, higher dividend).
  • Quality – how healthy the business looks (profit margins, growth, low debt).
  • Momentum – whether the share price has been trending upward lately.
  • Crowd activity – how busy the trading is, and how calm the price swings are (busier and calmer both score higher here).

Scores are relative, not absolute. A score of 80 means “better than about 80% of the other names on this list today”, not “80% good”. If everything on the list is expensive, the least expensive still scores high. The Total is a weighted mix of the four scores, and the # is its rank.

Halal badge. Green means it passed an automated halal screen, orange means a mixed result that needs a closer look, and “compliant ETF” means the fund’s own Shariah board screens it. The screening is automated and is not a religious ruling.

What this report is not. It describes what the market did and which measurable factors line up with it. It does not predict prices and is not financial advice. A high rank is a reason to look closer, not a reason to buy.
